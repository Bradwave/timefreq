About Time-Frequency
expand_more

This tool visualizes the Time-Frequency characteristics of a signal using the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT).

Unlike the standard Fourier Transform which shows global frequency content, the STFT breaks the signal into small time windows to show how frequencies change over time.

Spectrogram (Bottom): A heat map where the horizontal axis is Time, the vertical axis is Frequency, and color intensity represents Magnitude.

Window Functions:
• Gaussian: Smooth standard deviation, minimizes spectral leakage.
• Square: Sharp cutoff, good time resolution but causes spectral leakage (ringing).

Uncertainty Principle:
• Wide Window: Better Frequency resolution, worse Time resolution.
• Narrow Window: Better Time resolution, worse Frequency resolution.
